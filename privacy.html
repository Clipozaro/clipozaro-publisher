Privacy Policy

Clipozaro Publisher

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Clipozaro Publisher manages and publishes short-form gaming videos to authorized TikTok and YouTube accounts.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, filenames, captions, hashtags, upload IDs, publishing statuses, timestamps, and technical logs.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, publish authorized content, track publishing results, prevent duplicate uploads, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Data Storage and Security

The publishing workflow operates through a locally hosted n8n installation. Credentials, configuration, and publishing records are stored locally, subject to the security of the hosting environment.

5. Data Sharing

Information required for publishing is transmitted to TikTok, YouTube, and their associated API services. Clipozaro Publisher does not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Publishing records are retained as needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. Users may request deletion of information controlled by the application by contacting the operator. Connected-account access may be revoked through the relevant platform's settings.

7. Third-Party Services

TikTok and YouTube process information according to their respective privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or data-handling practices.

9. Contact
